## Research Methodology

<details>
<summary><b>Reconnaissance and Analysis</b></summary>

```text
01  RECONNAISSANCE
    Identify the attack surface and exposed functionality.

02  ANALYSIS
    Examine application behavior, trust boundaries, and controls.

03  VALIDATION
    Test hypotheses and establish reproducible evidence.

04  IMPACT ASSESSMENT
    Determine realistic security consequences and attack paths.

05  REPORTING
    Document evidence, impact, and remediation guidance.

06  DISCLOSURE
    Coordinate with maintainers and follow disclosure policies.
```

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Research Principles</b></summary>

```text
[+] Evidence over assumptions
[+] Manual validation over blind automation
[+] Impact over vulnerability labels
[+] Reproducibility over speculation
[+] Authorization before exploitation
[+] Responsible disclosure over public exposure
```

</details>
